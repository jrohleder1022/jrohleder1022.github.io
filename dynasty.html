How To Read This Site

Every ranking on this site is expressed as Dynasty Desk Value (DDV), a 0–1000 scale editorial model built for this format only: 12 teams, PPR, one starting QB. It blends age, situation, opportunity and current NFL news (rosters and headlines pulled live via ESPN) into a single number. It is not a scrape of KeepTradeCut, FantasyCalc, or any other live market — treat it as one well-reasoned opinion, refreshed as of July 28, 2026, and sanity-check big decisions against the live consensus tools linked below.

The Rankings and Startup ADP tabs both have a Dynasty / Redraft format toggle. Dynasty weighs age and long-term outlook; Redraft (regular, season-long, 12-team PPR) ignores age entirely and ranks purely on this year's expected role — proven veterans move up, unproven rookies and unclear backfields move down.

Draft Strategy

Startup Draft Priorities

  • In 1QB, quarterback is streamable — do not reach for one in round 1–2 unless it's a true top-5 rusher (Daniels, Allen, Jackson). Elite young RB/WR outproduce mid QBs on your bench.
  • Spend your first 4–5 picks on 23-and-under WR/RB with a clear path to touches. Age is the scarcest resource in dynasty.
  • Tight end is a wasteland after the top 5–6 names. Either pay up early for a true weekly starter or ignore the position until the last few rounds.
  • Rookie picks are lottery tickets, not promises — value a 1st-round rookie pick like a low-end WR3/RB3, not a guaranteed hit.
  • Handcuffing (drafting the backup to your own RB1) is a contender's move, not a rebuilder's. Rebuilders should draft the best player available instead.

Glossary
DDV — Dynasty Desk Value. This site's 0–1000 value score for a 12-team PPR 1QB league.
ADP — Average Draft Position: where a player actually gets picked, which can diverge from true value (see the Buy-Low board).
Startup Draft — the one-time draft that fills an entire new dynasty roster from scratch, players and rookies together.
Rookie Draft — the annual draft of just that year's incoming rookie class, held every subsequent season.
Superflex — a format allowing a 2nd starting QB, which pushes QB value far higher than in 1QB leagues like this one.
TEP — Tight End Premium: bonus points per reception for TEs, which raises the position's dynasty value when in effect.
Contender Window — the 1–2 seasons a roster's talent and age are best suited to win a championship.
Buy-Low / Sell-High — acquiring an undervalued asset before its price recovers, or trading an overvalued one before it falls.
Handcuff — the backup running back who would inherit a starting role if the starter is hurt.
Redraft (Regular League) — a season-long league that drafts a brand new roster every year; no keepers, no rookie picks, no dynasty value. Use the Redraft toggle on Rankings/ADP for this view.
